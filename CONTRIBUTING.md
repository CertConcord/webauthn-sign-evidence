# Contributing

Contributions should identify the selected upstream draft commit, exact input bytes, expected and actual behavior, and validation limits. Keep proposed application evidence rules distinct from WebAuthn extension text and from demonstrated authenticator behavior. Do not submit real credentials, identity data or private keys.

Original specification text is offered under CC BY 4.0, software under Apache-2.0, and original synthetic vectors under CC0. Identify third-party material and preserve its attribution and license.
